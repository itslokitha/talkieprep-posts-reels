"I always stay calm under pressure." So does every other candidate.

Behavioural questions aren't asking what kind of person you are. They're asking what you did, once.
A claim ("hard worker", "team player") can't be checked, so it doesn't stick. One real moment can.
Give them a moment, your action, and the result. Then say it out loud, because a story you've only written falls apart when you're nervous.
