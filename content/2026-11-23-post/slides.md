## 6/8 — Why B lands
- Label: WHY B LANDS
- Headline: B doesn't say "calm". / **It shows calm.** (teal)
- 1 A moment — One real shift. When, where, what went wrong.
- 2 Your action — What *you* did, step by step. "I", not "we".
- 3 The result — What changed. Plus what you do differently now.

## 7/8 — Takeaway
- Label: THE TAKEAWAY
- Headline: Practise saying it, / **not writing it.** (teal)

- ✓ Say it again until it sounds like you
- Body: A story you've only read falls apart when you're nervous.
