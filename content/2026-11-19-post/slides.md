## 4/8 — Line 1 · TECHNICAL
- Headline: Technical. / **Can you actually do it?**
- The line in the ad: Experience with electronic health records
- The question it becomes: "Walk me through how you document a patient handover."
- Note: They want the steps, in order. Not "I'm good with computers."

## 5/8 — Line 2 · NON-TECHNICAL
- Headline: Non-technical. / **Will it work for you?**
- The line in the ad: Rotating shifts, including nights
- The question it becomes: "How do you manage your energy on a run of nights?"
- Note: They want your real routine. Sleep, food, the drive home. Be specific.

## 6/8 — Line 3 · BEHAVIOURAL
- Headline: Behavioural. / **Prove it with a story.**
- The line in the ad: Works calmly under pressure
- The question it becomes: "Tell me about a time a shift went wrong. What did you do?"
- Note: They want one real moment. Not "I always stay calm."

## 7/8 — Takeaway
- Label: THE TAKEAWAY
- Headline: Every line in the ad / **is a question.**
- Technical — Can you do the work?
- Non-technical — Will the job fit your life?
- Behavioural — What did you do last time?
- Body: Then say each answer **out loud**. Not in your head.
