# Caption — 2026-11-19 POST

You prepared for "Tell me about yourself." They asked about line four of the job ad.

Generic question lists give you generic answers. The job ad is more useful: every line in it is a question waiting to happen.
A skill becomes a technical question. A shift pattern becomes a non-technical one. "Works calmly under pressure" becomes "Tell me about a time…"
Read the ad line by line, write the question each line hides, then say your answers out loud.
