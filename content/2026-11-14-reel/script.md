# Reel script — 2026-11-14 REEL (Day 3)

**Idea:** You don't run out of ideas. You run out of structure. (IELTS Speaking Part 2)
**Series tag:** EP. 01
**Length:** ~28 seconds · 9:16 (1080×1920)
**Format:** TalkiePrep screen recording (Part 2 practice, live transcript + timer) → timer running out → fix as chips → rerun.
**Cover-frame headline:** 1:04. / **Nothing left to say.** (coral) — see `cover.html`

**Music mood:** quiet, tense ticking pulse for 0–8s (low synth, clock tick on the beat), drops out for one beat of silence at the stall, then a light, steady minimal beat from 8s to the end (calm, forward-moving, no big drop). Keep music low under the spoken audio.

**Audio note:** the spoken answers are a staged demo recorded by the team (use an actor or team member), not a real user. Don't caption it as a customer.

**Safe area:** keep all key text above the bottom 300px and inside ~80px side margins.
