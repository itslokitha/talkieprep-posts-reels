1:04. Nothing left to say. Sound familiar?

That silence in IELTS Part 2 usually isn't a vocabulary problem. It's a prep problem.
If you spend your 1 minute writing full sentences, you say them in about 40 seconds and you're done.
Write 4 stops instead, keywords only: where it is, what it looks like, one specific moment, why it matters to you.
Each stop gives you something to talk about, not something to read out.
