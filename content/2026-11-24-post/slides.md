## 2/8 — The question
- Headline: The question. / **Straight from the ad.**
- The line in the ad: Works calmly under pressure
- The interviewer asks: "Tell me about a time a shift went wrong. What did you do?"
- Body: Sample nursing job ad from our last post. Two candidates answer.

## 3/8 — Answer A (coral marks)
- "I [always stay calm] under pressure. I'm a [very hard worker] and a [team player]. In nursing, things go wrong [all the time], so I just stay focused and [do my best] for the patients."
- Chips: ✕ No moment · ✕ No action · ✕ No result
- Body: Five claims. **Zero proof.** The question asked for "a time".

## 4/8 — Answer B (highlighted in three parts)
- "[On a night shift last winter, two of my patients got worse within ten minutes.] [I called the charge nurse for one, stayed with the other, and kept my notes as I went.] [Both were stable by morning. Now I raise early concerns at handover.]"
- Chips: ✓ A moment · ✓ Your action · ✓ The result
- Body: Never says "calm". **Shows it.**

## 5/8 — Comment prompt
- Headline: A or B? / **Comment before you scroll.**
- Mini cards: A "I always stay calm under pressure…" · B "On a night shift last winter…"
- Body: Then be honest: which one is closer to **what you'd say?** 👇

## 6/8 — Why B lands
- Label: WHY B LANDS
- Headline: Three parts. / **Under a minute.**
- 1 A moment — When, where, what went wrong.
- 2 Your action — What **you** did. "I", not "we".
- 3 The result — What changed. What you do now.

## 7/8 — Takeaway
- Label: THE TAKEAWAY
- Headline: Practise saying it, / **not writing it.**
- Card: On paper, you write B. Out loud, nervous, you drift back to A.
- Body: Say your story **three times** before the interview. Out loud. Same three parts.
