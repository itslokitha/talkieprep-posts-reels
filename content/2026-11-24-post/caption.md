"I always stay calm under pressure" is a claim. The interviewer asked for a time.

Behavioural questions come straight from the job ad. "Works calmly under pressure" becomes "Tell me about a time a shift went wrong."
Answer A lists qualities. Answer B tells one moment: what happened, what you did, what changed. It never says "calm". It shows it.
The catch: most people write B and then say A, because nerves pull you back to safe, general lines. So say your story out loud, more than once, before the real thing.
