# Reel script — 2026-11-15 REEL (Day 3)

**Idea:** You don't run out of ideas. You run out of structure. (IELTS Speaking Part 2)
**Angle:** Timer running out. A Part 2 practice stalls at 1:04, then the same answer is rerun
with a 4-stop route and lands past 1:45.
**Length:** ~27 seconds · 9:16 · 1080×1920
**Cover-frame headline:** Nothing left to say at 1:04? / **Fix the prep, not the ideas.** (teal)
**Music mood:** Low, ticking minimal beat (clock-like hi-hat) for the stall. Cut to silence for
the 4-second pause. Lighter, steady pulse comes in on the rerun. No vocals.

Safe zone: keep all key text above the bottom 300px and inside ~80px side margins.

## Production notes
- Use a real TalkiePrep practice recording for both takes (same speaker, same cue card:
  "Describe a place you like to visit."). Don't stage a score on screen; show only the timer
  and live transcript.
- The stall must feel real: keep the full 4 seconds of silence. That pause is the hook.
- Captions on all spoken lines (many viewers watch muted).
- End-frame prompt doubles as the comment driver: viewers reply with their time.
