The cue card

Describe a place you like to visit.

You should say:

  • where it is
  • how often you go there
  • what you do there

and explain why you like it.

⏱ 1 min to prepare🎙 Speak for 1–2 min

One minute of prep. Most people spend it on the wrong thing.
