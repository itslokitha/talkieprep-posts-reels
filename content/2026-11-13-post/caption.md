You didn't run out of ideas in Part 2. You ran out of structure.

Most people spend the 1 minute of prep writing full sentences. Then they say them, and there's nothing left.
Three sentences = about 40 seconds. Then the silence.
Write 4 stops instead, keywords only: where it is, what it looks like, one specific moment, why it matters to you.
Each stop is something to talk about, not something to read out.

📌 Save this for your next Part 2 practice.

👇 Be honest: how long do you usually last before the silence hits? Under 1 minute, 1–1:30, or the full 2?
