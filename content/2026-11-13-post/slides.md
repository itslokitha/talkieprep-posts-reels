## 2/8 — The cue card
- Label: THE CUE CARD
- Card: **Describe a place you like to visit.**
  You should say:
  - where it is
  - how often you go there
  - what you do there
  - and explain why you like it.
- Timer chips: ⏱ 1 min to prepare · 🎙 speak for 1–2 min
- Line: One minute of prep. Most people spend it on the wrong thing.

## 3/8 — What I prepared
- Headline: What I prepared. / **Full sentences.** (coral)
- Label: MY PREP NOTES
- Note card (handwritten feel):
  "My favourite place is a beach near my city.
  I go there often with my family.
  I like it because it is very relaxing."
- Coral tag: 60 seconds spent writing 3 sentences.

## 4/8 — What came out
- Headline: What came out. / **0:41, then silence.** (coral)
- Label: PRACTICE TRANSCRIPT
- Transcript (coral boxes on fillers):
  "My favourite place is a beach near my city. I go there, **um**, often with my family. I like it because it is, **uh**, very relaxing. And… **like**… yeah."
- Silence chip: ● 4 seconds of silence and counting
- Timer bar: 0:41 / 2:00

## 5/8 — The fix
- Headline: Write stops, / **not sentences.** (teal)
- Struck-through (coral): ~~"My favourite place is a beach near my city."~~
- Label: A 4-STOP ROUTE · KEYWORDS ONLY
- 1. Where / when → beach, 20 min, Sundays
- 2. What it looks like → grey sand, food trucks
- 3. One specific moment → storm, ran to car
- 4. Why it matters → only place I switch off

## 6/8 — Same answer, rerun
- Headline: Same place. / **Still talking at 1:50.** (teal)
- Label: PRACTICE TRANSCRIPT · RERUN
- Stop chips with lines:
  - ① "It's a beach about twenty minutes from my flat. I go most Sundays…"
  - ② "The sand is grey, not golden, and there are food trucks along the road…"
  - ③ "Last spring a storm came in out of nowhere and we ran to the car…"
  - ④ "Honestly, it's the only place where I stop checking my phone…"
- Timer bar: 1:50 / 2:00 (teal)
