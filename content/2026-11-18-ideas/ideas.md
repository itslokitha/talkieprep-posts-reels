### Your interview questions are already written. They're in the job ad.

- **Product:** TalkiePrep Interview — interview.talkieprep.com (new). You paste the whole job
  description and your information; the AI pulls out the technical, non-technical and
  behavioural questions for that job and then interviews you on them.
- **Exam(s) and language:** Job interviews, not an exam (English). Same audience, next step:
  people moving for study, work or immigration (nursing, Canada, Germany) who now face an
  interview in their second language. Series continues as EP. 02 for the reel.
- **Why it will land:** People prepare from generic "top 50 interview questions" lists and
  rehearse "tell me about yourself" until it's perfect. Then the interviewer asks about one
  specific line in the job description — a tool, a shift pattern, a "works well under pressure" —
  and they freeze. The fear: "I prepared, and they still asked something I didn't expect."
  The fix: the job ad tells you what they'll ask. Read every line as a question, and say the
  answers out loud before the real thing.
- **Accuracy notes (check before Day 2):** Only describe what's confirmed above (paste job
  description + your info → technical, non-technical and behavioural questions → spoken
  interview). Don't mention feedback, scores, transcripts, languages, pricing or a free trial
  for the interview product unless you've added that to this file. Use a made-up, generic
  job ad (no real employer names). CTA goes to interview.talkieprep.com and talkieprep.com.
